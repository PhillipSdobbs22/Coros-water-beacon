COROS Water Recovery Mode

Where the beacon fires, and where it stays dark

A watch that goes into the water only lights up if it sinks past the trigger depth. Set the trigger too deep and the beacon is silent in exactly the water where a lost watch is still findable. Drag the two sliders and watch that happen.
